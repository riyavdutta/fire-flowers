# Fire Flowers 花火

In Japanese, fireworks are *hanabi*, literally "fire flowers". This is a small game that takes the name at its word. Part of [Riya Dutta's portfolio](https://riya-folio.vercel.app/).

- **Night:** launch fireworks over a river. Choose from 菊 chrysanthemum, 牡丹 peony, 柳 willow, 輪 ring and 千輪 thousand. The sky and the water light up together.
- **Day:** the same five shells burst as real flowers instead: chrysanthemum, peony, 藤 wisteria, 桜 cherry blossom and 紫陽花 hydrangea. The petals drift down and float off on the river (花筏, a flower raft).

Pick a shell and one or two colours, then press and hold in the sky and let go. Holding longer makes a bigger bloom. Each mode has five commissions to match (place, size, flower and colour), and you can also just play.

It's one self-contained `index.html` with no build step and no dependencies. It runs on canvas and Web Audio, and every sound is made in the browser.

For testing, `?demo=1&t=2.2` fast-forwards a small show and `?mode=day` opens in day mode.
